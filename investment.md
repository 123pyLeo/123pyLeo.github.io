    <h1>Business Reflections</h1>
    <p>
      I’m drawn to the moment an idea leaves the lab and becomes part of
      someone’s everyday life. Someday, I may build a company of my own.
      I want to understand how something one person imagines can come to matter to many.
    </p>
    <p>
      That curiosity took me to a buyout fund. I came away with a belief:
      the value of a technology becomes clearer in the commitments people make
      around it: the habits they change, the trust they place, and the money
      they keep spending. I’m interested in how those commitments turn a
      promising idea into a lasting business.
    </p>
  </section>
